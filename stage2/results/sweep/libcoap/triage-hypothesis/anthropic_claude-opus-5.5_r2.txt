The clearest bug is at line 538. `address->length` is copied into the 256-byte static `addrstr` without being checked against `sizeof(addrstr)`. A host string of 256 bytes or more overflows the buffer. Even a host of exactly 256 bytes leaves `addrstr` without a NUL terminator, so `getaddrinfo` reads past its end. The AF_UNIX branch does check length, but the normal path does not.

Two weaker issues appear in the non-POSIX branches:
- **RIOT (lines 672 and 678):** `address->s` is passed to `netutils_get_ipv6`/`netutils_get_ipv4` as a C string. A `coap_str_const_t` is length-delimited and may not be NUL-terminated, so these calls can read out of bounds.
- **Contiki (lines 745 and 751):** `address->s` is likewise treated as a NUL-terminated string.
